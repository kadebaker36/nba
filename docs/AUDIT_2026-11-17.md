## Vision preserved

No passes, supporting weights, hard percentile admission thresholds or automatic final-selection scores were added. Own shooting earns credit. Raw opponent 3P% stays contextual. Specific shot-quality concessions are considered separately from total allowance. The shortlist may shrink to zero. Availability review uses an educated expected rotation, and players are reviewed one team at a time. A user challenge triggers a check of the same evidence rather than automatic agreement.

The method now explicitly treats defender distance as a limited opportunity proxy rather than a comprehensive defensive or shooter-adjusted prediction. Historical examples retain cutoff, units, coverage and public endpoint provenance, and cannot become permanent team preferences.
