# Volume and quality can point in different directions

Historical pregame reasoning through November 16, 2026. These examples illustrate the agreed method, not a backtest or current recommendation. Tracking was partial and early-season adjustments were unshrunk.

The source run covered 205 completed NBA games, 204 fully paired tracking games, 408 tracked team-games and 41,000 possessions; 103 tracked team-games had incomplete shot counts. Units below are attempts or expected makes per 100 possessions. P denotes a league percentile within the stated window. These observations are dated inputs, not permanent team preferences. Public source families are NBA's `leaguedashteamstats`, `leaguedashoppptshot` and `leaguegamelog` endpoints with regular-season filters and a November 16 cutoff. Exact request URLs and raw responses are retained in the historical shot-quality archive identified by docs/DATA_MAP.md.

## Portland at Minnesota: an initial offensive strength can fail later review

Portland attempted 43.1 threes per 100 possessions (P90), with 36.0% own shooting (P59). Minnesota allowed 35.0 attempts/100 (P12), falling to 34.1 (P3) over the last ten. Its opponent-adjusted allowance was -1.04 expected threes/100, or -1.66 with fully tracked team-games only.

High offensive volume earned research. The completed passes did not establish enough favorable defensive concession to advance the team. Possible extra minutes after a creator absence did not independently restore the case. Specific shooters might still score, but selecting those players could not retroactively qualify the team. Do not fill a shortlist vacancy automatically.

## Clippers versus Utah: low total allowance is not an automatic veto

The Clippers shot 39.1% season and 38.6% recent (P97 in each window). They attempted 36.5 threes/100; Utah allowed 34.8 (P7). Utah also conceded 19.29 wide-open threes/100 tracked possessions, versus a 17.80 league average. Its total adjusted allowance was -0.58 expected threes/100, with a -1.18 full-tracking sensitivity.

There was favorable offensive shooting, a specific conceded shot-type opening, and unfavorable overall volume/adjusted evidence. Those facts must be considered together. The broad count alone did not establish that relevant players would lack sufficient opportunities.

A rough screen, mean(36.5, 34.8) multiplied by mean team pace/100, yielded about 35.7 attempts, less than one below the offense's usual game volume. This arithmetic is illustrative, not a fitted forecast or an additional bonus. High own shooting also is not a guarantee of stable conversion.

The final team judgment was provisional advancement to player review as the third candidate, at lower conviction. The next question was whether expected individual shooters received the conceded looks at sufficient minutes and frequency. Failure of that test could still remove every player. Broad team accuracy and a raw wide-open allowance did not yet establish a particular player over.

## Decision discipline

- Separate volume, shot quality and conversion. Use absolute magnitudes beside percentiles.
- Record both the advantage and its strongest contradiction in one assessment.
- Explain changes using new data, corrected calculations or corrected interpretation. Do not swing between automatic rejection and automatic rescue.
- Use no fixed shortlist quota and no single-metric veto.
